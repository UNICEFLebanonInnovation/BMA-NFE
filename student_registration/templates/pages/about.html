About CLM+
System Information and Capabilities
What is CLM+?

CLM+ (Child Labour Monitoring System) is a comprehensive platform designed for the integration of student and beneficiary registration, education tracking, well-being analysis, and attendance monitoring. It empowers centers, partners, and administrators to seamlessly manage data related to education services, child protection, and psychosocial support.

Key Features
  • Registration Flow: Secure, role-based, multi-step registration wizard for beneficiaries.
  • Education & Attendance: Real-time tracking of classes, educational progress, and daily attendance.
  • Wellbeing Analysis: Integrated dashboard visualizations tracking psychological support, educational improvements, and the impact of child labor on attendance.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Distinct access levels ensuring data privacy and correct scope for Centers, Partners, and Global Administrators.
  • Geographic Mapping: Interactive maps for visualizing center locations and metadata.
  • Data Export & Reporting: Advanced asynchronous data export capabilities in both CSV and XLSX formats.
