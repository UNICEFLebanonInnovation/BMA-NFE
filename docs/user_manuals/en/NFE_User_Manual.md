# NFE Module – User Manual

**BMA Platform – Non-Formal Education Sector**

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

This manual explains, step by step, how to use the **NFE (Non-Formal Education) module** of the BMA platform. It is written for center staff who register children, record the services they receive, track daily attendance, manage teachers and consult the dashboards. No technical knowledge is required.

Each step is illustrated with a screenshot taken from the system. Screens may differ slightly depending on your role: a **center user** sees only the children of their own center, a **partner user** sees all the centers of their organisation, and **UNICEF users** have a read-only view across all partners.

The data shown in the screenshots is demonstration data only.

## 1. Getting Started – Login and Navigation

Open Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox and go to the platform address given to you by your coordinator. Accounts are created by the system administrator; there is no self-registration.

1. Open the login page. Enter your **username** and **password**.

    ![Figure 1](../screenshots/en/00_login_empty.png)

    *Figure 1 – The login page*

2. Click **Sign in**. If the details are correct you are taken to your home page.

    ![Figure 2](../screenshots/en/00_login_filled.png)

    *Figure 2 – Login form with the credentials filled in*

3. The **home page** shows today's registrations, this week's registrations, the centers reporting, the average attendance, a 14-day registration trend and your recent data exports. The **New Registration** card opens the registration form directly.

    ![Figure 3](../screenshots/en/nfe_01_home.png)

    *Figure 3 – The home page after login*

4. To change the interface language, click the **globe icon** in the top bar and choose **English** or **العربية**. The page reloads in the chosen language with a right-to-left layout for Arabic.

    ![Figure 4](../screenshots/en/nfe_02_language_menu.png)

    *Figure 4 – Language menu*

5. Click your **name** in the top-right corner to open the user menu: **Profile**, **Change Password** and **Logout**.

    ![Figure 5](../screenshots/en/nfe_03_user_menu.png)

    *Figure 5 – User menu*


| Menu entry | What it opens |
|---|---|
| Home | The home page with the daily summary cards |
| Dashboard | The NFE operational dashboard (registrations, gender, nationality, age groups…) |
| Registration | The beneficiary list, the registration form and the child profiles |
| Attendance | Daily attendance entry for your center |
| Centers | The list of centers you can access |
| Teachers | The teacher list and the teacher form |
| Pivot Builder | Build your own cross-tabulations of the registration data |
| Learning Outcome | Learning outcome and socio-economic analysis |
| Teacher Dashboard | Teacher demographics, assignments and training |
| Attendance Heatmap | Attendance patterns across the year |
| Centers Map | Map and profile of the centers |
| User Guide | The documentation inside the system |

> **Note:** For security, the system logs you out automatically after a period of inactivity. Save your work regularly. After five failed login attempts in five minutes the account is temporarily blocked: wait a few minutes and try again.

## 2. The Beneficiary List

The beneficiary list shows every child registered in your center (or in all the centers of your partner organisation). Open it with **Registration** in the side menu.

1. The list shows one row per registration: round, unique ID, names, gender, age, birthday, nationality and programme. Click a column header to sort by that column. Scroll horizontally to see all the columns.

    ![Figure 6](../screenshots/en/nfe_10_list.png)

    *Figure 6 – The beneficiary list*

2. Click **Search** to open the filter panel. Filter by first, father, last or mother name, unique ID, gender, nationality, round or programme, then click **Apply Filters**. **Clear All** removes the filters.

    ![Figure 7](../screenshots/en/nfe_11_list_search.png)

    *Figure 7 – The search and filter panel*

3. Click **Actions** on a row to open the row menu: **View Profile**, **Upload Photo**, **Edit Information** and **Delete Record**. Deleting asks for a confirmation and cannot be undone.

    ![Figure 8](../screenshots/en/nfe_12_list_actions.png)

    *Figure 8 – The Actions menu of a registration*


> **Note:** The **Total absence** column counts the days the child was marked absent in the current round. It is a quick way to spot children at risk of dropping out.

## 3. Registering a New Child

Registration is done through a **3-step wizard**: Identity, Caregivers and Review. The child is only saved when you click **Complete Registration** on the last step. Fields marked with a red asterisk (*) are mandatory.

Open the form with **New Registration** on the home page, or from the beneficiary list.

As soon as you type the child's name and date of birth, the **Potential Matches** panel at the top of the form searches the existing records. If the same child is already registered, the panel turns red, shows the existing record and blocks the **Next Step** button. In that case do **not** create a new record: open the existing profile, or use **Select & Re-register** to add the child to a new round.

![Figure 9](../screenshots/en/nfe_21_reg_duplicate_warning.png)

*Figure 9 – The duplicate check runs while you type the identity*

1. Fill in the **Basic Identity Information**: child photo (optional), first name, father's name, family name, date of birth (day, month, year), country and place of birth, gender, mother's full name, nationality and the **non-formal education programme** (BLN Level 1, 2 or 3). Names must be written in **Arabic letters** exactly as on the official document.

    ![Figure 10](../screenshots/en/nfe_20_reg_step1_empty.png)

    *Figure 10 – Step 1 – the empty identity form*

2. Fill in the **Residential Address**: governorate, district, cadaster and village. Each list is loaded after you choose the previous level. Then answer the **disability** question in **Status & Background**.
3. Check the data and click **Next Step**. If a mandatory field is missing, the field is highlighted in red and a **Verification Required** message lists what must be corrected.

    ![Figure 11](../screenshots/en/nfe_22_reg_step1_filled.png)

    *Figure 11 – Step 1 completed*


| Field | Guidance |
|---|---|
| First / Father / Family name | In Arabic letters, as written on the ID document |
| Date of birth | Select day, month and year from the lists |
| Country and place of birth | As written on the official document |
| Mother full name | Required, in Arabic letters |
| Nationality | Choose from the list; if "Other" specify it in the next field |
| Non-formal education programme | BLN Level 1, BLN Level 2 or BLN Level 3 |
| Formal education unique student ID | Optional – the ID used in the formal school, if any |
| Governorate / District / Cadaster / Village | Where the child currently lives |
| Disability or special need | Required; if "Other" describe it |

1. Enter the **primary phone number** of the caregiver in the format **XX-XXXXXX** (for example 03-123456). This is the number the center uses to contact the family.

    ![Figure 12](../screenshots/en/nfe_23_reg_step2_empty.png)

    *Figure 12 – Step 2 – caregiver contact*

2. Click **Next Step**. Use **Previous** at any time to go back and correct a value.

    ![Figure 13](../screenshots/en/nfe_24_reg_step2_filled.png)

    *Figure 13 – Step 2 completed*


1. Review the summary: full name, date of birth, country and place of birth, nationality, gender, phone number, programme and address. If anything is wrong click **Previous** and correct it. **Reset Form** clears everything.

    ![Figure 14](../screenshots/en/nfe_25_reg_step3_review.png)

    *Figure 14 – Step 3 – final review*

2. Click **Complete Registration**. The message "Your data has been sent successfully to the server" confirms the registration and the child's **profile page** opens.

    ![Figure 15](../screenshots/en/nfe_26_reg_submitted.png)

    *Figure 15 – The profile page right after registration*


> **Note:** The center, partner and round are filled in automatically from your account. If your account has no center assigned, a warning is displayed on the list: ask your administrator to link your account to a center.

## 4. The Child Profile

The profile is the central record of a child. Open it from the beneficiary list with **Actions → View Profile**.

1. The **header** shows the photo, the full name, the unique ID, the date of birth with the age, the address, the partner, the center and the current round. The buttons on the right open **Download Examination Card (Word)** and **Edit Profile**.

    ![Figure 16](../screenshots/en/nfe_30_profile_header.png)

    *Figure 16 – Profile header*

2. The **Personal Info** tab groups the core identity, the household and care information and the social and economic situation.

    ![Figure 17](../screenshots/en/nfe_30_profile_full.png)

    *Figure 17 – Personal Info tab*

3. The **Education situation** tab shows the current enrolment (educational level, programme, round, section, registration date), the education history, the pre/post grading of the programme and the referral card.

    ![Figure 18](../screenshots/en/nfe_31_profile_education_tab.png)

    *Figure 18 – Education situation tab*

4. The **Attendances** tab shows the total teaching days, the days attended, the absences and the absences of the current month. Click a month in **Monthly History** to see the detailed daily records.

    ![Figure 19](../screenshots/en/nfe_32_profile_attendance_tab.png)

    *Figure 19 – Attendances tab*

5. The **Exceptional Exam** tab shows the exam details recorded for the registration, or a button to add them (see section 6).

    ![Figure 20](../screenshots/en/nfe_33_profile_exam_tab.png)

    *Figure 20 – Exceptional Exam tab*


1. Click **Edit Profile** (or **Actions → Edit Information** in the list). The same 3-step form opens with the saved values. Change what is needed, go through the steps and click **Complete Registration** to save.

    ![Figure 21](../screenshots/en/nfe_62_edit_profile.png)

    *Figure 21 – Editing a registration*


1. Click the **camera icon** on the profile photo, or **Actions → Upload Photo** in the list. Choose a clear, recent photo of the child (JPG or PNG) and click **Save**.

    ![Figure 22](../screenshots/en/nfe_60_photo_upload.png)

    *Figure 22 – Photo upload form*

2. The profile reopens with the new photo. The photo is also embedded in the Excel export and printed on the examination card.

    ![Figure 23](../screenshots/en/nfe_61_photo_saved.png)

    *Figure 23 – Photo saved*


Click **Download Examination Card (Word)** in the profile header to download an editable Word card with the child's photo, round, registration ID, partner, full name, date of birth, nationality and governorate. Correct the profile first if anything is wrong: each download reads the saved record again.

## 5. Education Situation and Round Enrolment

The **education situation** records the child's educational level at registration and the programme, round and class section the child attends. It is required before the child appears in the attendance sheets.

1. Open the profile, go to the **Education situation** tab and click **Add** (or **Edit** if a situation already exists).

    ![Figure 24](../screenshots/en/nfe_40_education_form_empty.png)

    *Figure 24 – The education situation form*

2. Choose the **educational level when registering**, the **round**, the **programme**, the **class section** and the **date of registration in the round**. If the child dropped out of a school, give the dropout date.

    ![Figure 25](../screenshots/en/nfe_41_education_form_filled.png)

    *Figure 25 – Education situation completed*

3. Click **Save Education Data**. The profile reopens on the Education situation tab with the new enrolment and a new line in the **Education History**.

    ![Figure 26](../screenshots/en/nfe_42_education_saved.png)

    *Figure 26 – The saved education situation*


1. When a new round starts, open the **Register in a New Round** page for the child (from the duplicate warning's **Select & Re-register** button, or from the **New Round** button in the profile header when your role has it). The **Registration History** lists the rounds the child already attended.

    ![Figure 27](../screenshots/en/nfe_34_new_round_form.png)

    *Figure 27 – Register in a new round*

2. Fill in the education situation for the new round and click **Save Education Data**. A new registration record is created for the current round; the previous rounds stay in the history.

> **Note:** The programmes offered depend on the child's age. If the programme you need is not listed, check the date of birth.

## 6. Exceptional Exam

Children who sit the exceptional examination need an exam record on their registration. Each registration has one exam record.

1. Open the profile, go to the **Exceptional Exam** tab and click **Add Exceptional Exam**. The residence, country and place of birth and the programme are taken from the registration and displayed in grey.

    ![Figure 28](../screenshots/en/nfe_50_exam_form_empty.png)

    *Figure 28 – The exceptional exam form*

2. Check the **grade applying for the examination** (derived from the programme level), choose the **mathematics examination language** (Arabic, English or French) and the **language examination** (English or French).

    ![Figure 29](../screenshots/en/nfe_51_exam_form_filled.png)

    *Figure 29 – Exam form completed*

3. Click **Save**. The message "Exceptional exam saved" appears and the tab shows the recorded answers. Use **Edit Exceptional Exam** to correct them later.

    ![Figure 30](../screenshots/en/nfe_52_exam_saved.png)

    *Figure 30 – The saved exam details*


> **Note:** If the residence or programme shown is wrong, correct the child's registration first (Edit Profile) and then come back to the exam form.

## 7. Daily Attendance

Attendance is recorded per day, per programme and per class section. Open it with **Attendance** in the side menu.

1. The date defaults to **today**. Choose the **Education Program**, the **Round** and the **Section**.

    ![Figure 31](../screenshots/en/nfe_70_attendance_page.png)

    *Figure 31 – The attendance page*

2. Click **Load**. The children enrolled in that programme and section appear on the right, each with **Attended** and **Absent** buttons.

    ![Figure 32](../screenshots/en/nfe_71_attendance_loaded.png)

    *Figure 32 – Children loaded for the selected class*

3. Children are marked **Attended** by default. For each absent child click **Absent** and choose the **absence reason** (Sick, Transportation or Other – describe it in the text box).

    ![Figure 33](../screenshots/en/nfe_72_attendance_marked.png)

    *Figure 33 – Absences marked with their reason*

4. Click **Save**. A **Success** message confirms the attendance was saved. You can reload the same day later to correct it.

    ![Figure 34](../screenshots/en/nfe_73_attendance_saved.png)

    *Figure 34 – Attendance saved*

5. If the center was closed, select **Day Off** instead of marking children, choose the **closing reason** and save.

    ![Figure 35](../screenshots/en/nfe_74_attendance_day_off.png)

    *Figure 35 – Recording a day off*


- Attendance can be recorded for today or for a past date; **future dates are refused**.
- A child who does not appear in the list has no education situation for that programme and section (see section 5), or the registration is not active.
- **Download** gives a CSV file of the list currently loaded on screen.

## 8. Teachers

Every teacher or facilitator of the center must be recorded. Open **Teachers** in the side menu.

1. The teacher list shows the names, gender, phone number, center, round and who created the record. Use **Search** to filter and **Export** to download the list. Click **Add Teacher** to create a record.

    ![Figure 36](../screenshots/en/nfe_80_teachers_list.png)

    *Figure 36 – The teacher list*

2. Fill in **Identity & Contact**: names in Arabic letters, gender, birth date, nationality, ID type and number, phone (format XX-XXXXXX) and email.

    ![Figure 37](../screenshots/en/nfe_81_teacher_form_empty.png)

    *Figure 37 – The teacher form*

3. Fill in **Assignment & Qualifications**: center, academic year, assignment, teaching hours, subjects provided, grade levels, training topics attended, years of experience and extra coaching. Add up to four supporting documents under **Required Documents**.

    ![Figure 38](../screenshots/en/nfe_82_teacher_form_filled.png)

    *Figure 38 – Teacher form completed*

4. Click **Complete Registration**. The teacher appears in the list.

    ![Figure 39](../screenshots/en/nfe_83_teacher_saved.png)

    *Figure 39 – Teacher saved*

5. To update a teacher use **Actions → Edit** in the list, change the values and save. **Delete** removes the teacher after confirmation.

    ![Figure 40](../screenshots/en/nfe_84_teacher_edit.png)

    *Figure 40 – Editing a teacher*

## 10. Exporting Data

Exports are started from the beneficiary list and prepared in the background, so you can keep working.

1. In the beneficiary list click **Export**. Choose the **children to export** (all the children you can access, or only the current filter results) and the **format**: CSV (text only), Excel with photographs, or Word examination cards (ZIP).

    ![Figure 42](../screenshots/en/nfe_13_export_modal.png)

    *Figure 42 – Export options*

2. Click **Start Export**. When the file is ready a message tells you the download starts automatically.

    ![Figure 43](../screenshots/en/nfe_14_export_started.png)

    *Figure 43 – Export completed*

3. Click the **bell icon** in the top bar to open **Recent Exports**. Each entry shows its status and lets you download the file again. **View All History** opens the full export history.

    ![Figure 44](../screenshots/en/nfe_15_export_history.png)

    *Figure 44 – Recent exports menu*


- Excel exports embed each child photo in the same row as the child and registration IDs.
- CSV files are UTF-8 with a byte-order mark, so Arabic names open correctly in Excel.
- Only the user who requested an export can download it.
- **Download Examination Cards** on the list prepares the Word cards of the selected children as one ZIP file.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| The Next Step button is disabled on the registration form. | A potential duplicate was found. Check the Potential Matches panel: open the existing profile or re-register the child in a new round instead of creating a new record. |
| My names are erased when I leave the field. | Names must be written in Arabic letters only. Latin letters and digits are removed automatically. |
| The phone number is refused. | Use the format XX-XXXXXX, for example 03-123456. |
| A child does not appear in the attendance list. | Add the education situation (programme, round and section) on the child profile, then reload the attendance sheet. |
| The district or village list is empty. | Select the governorate first, then the district. If the lists still do not load, click Try again or refresh the page. |
| I cannot see children from another center. | Center users only see their own center. Ask your coordinator if you need a wider scope. |
| I forgot my password. | There is no self-service reset. Contact your system administrator. |
| Who do I contact for support? | Your partner focal point first, then the BMA support team through your coordinator. |
